# 04 — Decode the four measurement registers for both SKUs

## Inputs

Consume `baseline`, `value-status` and `rtu-codec`. Apply [COMMON](COMMON.md). Use the audited [register map](../reference/protocol/registers.csv), [measurement/timing notes](../reference/measurement-and-configuration.md), [product limits](../reference/hardware.md) and [golden frames](../reference/protocol/modbus_vectors.csv). Produce `measurements`.

## Work

1. Expose `MeasurementKind::{ProcessedDistance, RealTimeDistance, Temperature, EchoTime}` through the installed public include. Map these selectors to processed distance (0x0100, mm), real-time distance (0x0101, mm), temperature (0x0102, tenths of °C) and echo time (0x010A, µs) respectively. Route every request through the bounded codec and every response through exact expected validation. A sample carries its explicit SKU, destination, originating measurement kind/register, raw uint16_t word, interpreted value availability/quality, units and owner-supplied completion time with separate validity. Receipt time is distinct from the unknown acoustic sampling instant. [Operation matrix](OPERATIONS.csv)
2. Apply rated distance limits of 20..3000 mm for SEN0591 and 30..5000 mm for SEN0592 inclusively. Preserve out-of-rated-range words for diagnostics and flag their quality. Treat `0x0000`, `0xFFFF` or any other candidate no-echo sentinel according to the same evidence rule. The manuals establish no missing-echo code. Scale supported temperature as raw tenths, report the documented 0.5 °C resolution and preserve unexpected quantization. Do not cast high words to signed or publish implausible subzero/high-word readings as established temperatures. Document quality criteria, using the operating band only as context. Keep echo duration as the primary result. Offer an explicitly approximate conversion `µs / 5.75` to mm without truncating through integer division.
3. Make timing metadata available by **model and register**. Preserve both published SEN0592 lower bounds for processed distance (190/300 ms) and for temperature and echo (5/40 ms). SEN0591 minima are 190 ms for processed and 5 ms for temperature and echo. Both SKUs give processed upper 750 ms, real-time 15..140 ms, temperature/echo upper 140 ms. Include SEN0591's four narrower range-level distance pairs from the reference and the broader table-1 bound used for scheduling. Metadata describes approximate sensor response. The owner accounts for serial time, turnaround, RTU frame completion and margin.
4. Distance and echo reads start distinct ranging cycles. Keep each FC03 request individually attributable. The temperature sampling instant is unspecified. Expose configuration readbacks separately from measurement results. Publish a new engineering value only for the intended live request and the applicable quality, including independent valid bits for different quantities. On a rejected reply keep the previous successful sample available with its original timestamp. Changing desired mode/SKU or reporting cached data must not start a read.
5. Give the caller a passive way to determine freshness per binding and measurement kind from its own monotonic `nowMs`, last valid timestamp and maximum age. Document the exact boundary and wrap-safe interval and distinguish never sampled, unknown age, fresh, stale and unusable data. A valid word outside the usable range can update the latest raw observation without replacing the last usable reading. Keep their identities and timestamps separate. Invalidate or re-key these caches when the binding changes. The application chooses its freshness threshold.

## Acceptance

- Exercise all four kinds on both profiles, including the manufacturer values 754 mm, 751 mm and 30.0 °C. Label the echo example as synthetic and check its raw and approximate results. Test exact SKU bounds, just outside each end, zero/0xFFFF, a high temperature word, a valid time of zero and an unknown timestamp.
- Demonstrate that a stale reading survives an exception, time-out or corrupt frame with its original timestamp and continuing age. Check the configured freshness boundary, the next millisecond, timer wrap and binding replacement. A responding device and a usable measurement can have different outcomes. Test a valid but out-of-rated-range reply without claiming disconnection. Make a same-shaped reply decode only in its declared live context.
- Verify model-specific table values, range-level estimates and all units against the register references. Run the native consumer and offline tests. Document uncertainty and the capture needed for a future change to the interpretation.
- For each of the four selectors on each SKU build an addressed request, parse it with its retained context and assert the selected result, raw word and units. Keep measurement decoding independent of UART output configuration. Stage 05 repeats the unit checks at both values of `Setting::UartUnit`. Stages 09–12 replay these cases through `read`, `watch`, `latest` and numeric `readreg` in both full examples. Preserve the single live read, bounded repetition or zero-transfer cache semantics of the chosen command.

## Delegation and self-audit

Apply the [stage audit](AUDIT.md). Have the reviewer interleave two measurement kinds with identical reply bytes and then inject a late reply. Verify raw word, units, last-good timestamp and freshness belong to the right binding on both SKUs.
